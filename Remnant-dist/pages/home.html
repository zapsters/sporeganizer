Sporeganizer

Where your tasks grow in order, not chaos.

The lightweight homework and class tracking app that helps your tasks stay on track and grow! With Sporeganizer, you'll stay fung-tual and on top of your assignments. Our mushroom-inspired design makes managing your workload feel natural and fun, without the hassle.

Features

  • Access your dashboard from anywhere.
  • Flawless sync between devices.
  • Easy connection to Google accounts.
  • Custom classes complete with days and times.
  • Homework and Class Management.
  • Dark and Light Mode Themes
